# Farshore Phase 3: list of changes

Part A lists what changed between the Phase 2 design (the October 8 arcade package, 15 screens and wireframes W01-W28) and the prototype that was tested. Part B lists changes made because of the usability test. Part B is completed only after the sessions, from the Issues sheet in the test data workbook.

Phase 2 personas are referenced as Maya (a gamer who cares about fairness), Daniel (a reader with limited technical comfort) and Rosa (skeptical of AI). They remain the Phase 1 sketches, not new research.

## Part A. Phase 2 design to tested prototype

| ID | Change | Why | Replaces in Phase 2 | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Five trials became eight: Lotus-Eaters maze rescue, Cyclops' Cave runner, Aeolus wind sailing, Circe's potion duel, Sirens' melody memory, Scylla and Charybdis paddle defense, Calypso's raft hopping, Ithaca archery. | Follows the Odyssey route more closely and gives each episode its own recognizable game. Lotus-Eaters, a maze with simple movement, now comes first. | S03 map with five shores; S05-S09 rounds | `src/lib/arcade/arcade.ts`, commits efcce87-1b8d804 |
| A2 | The AI rival is no longer optional. Every encounter needs a connected model, and there is no model-free mode. | The AI opponent is the core experience, not an add-on. A clear "AI rival offline" state with connection steps replaces the optional toggle. | S04 briefing "mode" choice and A1 optional planning | `src/ArcadeApp.tsx` rival panel; decision recorded 2026-10-26 |
| A3 | The model changed from Llama 3.2 3B to Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (Ollama `qwen3:4b-instruct`). Online accounts can use OpenAI, Groq or OpenRouter instead. | Better instruction and JSON-schema following (MMLU-Pro 69.6 vs 22.2) and no slow thinking phase, which matters for live commentary. Hosted providers help players without a strong laptop. | Phase 2 spike model | README "Connect the AI rival" |
| A4 | Live AI commentary was added: a Homeric bard and the rival comment on play, and the bard writes a short verse after each attempt. Lines are spoken with Kokoro-82M neural voices, with the browser voice as the fallback. | Makes the AI's presence felt during play rather than only before it. Players can switch commentary and voice off separately in Settings. | Not in Phase 2 (model was used only before a round) | commit fbf5cf2; `src/lib/arcade/commentary` |
| A5 | Explainable AI inspector: AI Insights (default) and AI Lab views over Cyclops' Cave, cycled with F3 or on-screen chips. They show the rival's state, scored candidates, the chosen action, a grounded reason and a fairness guarantee. | Directly answers the Phase 2 trust question: players (Maya, Rosa) can see why the rival acted and that it cannot make an unfair attack. | Phase 2 showed only the plan name before a round | commits d6db373, f9067cd; `src/components/ai/ai-inspector.tsx` |
| A6 | AI Lab (Professor) mode from the main menu runs any encounter at any difficulty, side by side, without touching the campaign save. | Lets an instructor or a curious player compare rival behavior. Supports the class demonstration. | No Phase 2 equivalent | commit d2d0301 |
| A7 | Gameplay moved from a canvas inside the page to a full-viewport 16:9 stage, with F for fullscreen and Escape to pause, and HUD overlays. | The highest-priority item in the redesign brief: the small embedded canvas competed with page chrome for space. | S05-S09 layout, S13 pause | `src/components/play/game-stage.tsx`, `docs/UI_DESIGN.md` |
| A8 | Cyclops' Cave became a 3D three-lane runner (three.js), and Ithaca became a 3D great hall for the archery contest. A 2D fallback is kept for devices without WebGL. | Stronger sense of place and clearer depth cues for dodging and aiming. | S05 grid stealth, S09 archery | commits 4090a08, 14ae287 |
| A9 | Online accounts: email/password and Google sign-in, MongoDB saves across devices, and private per-account AI provider settings. Guests still save on the device. | Lets a signed-in player continue on another device and use a hosted model without a strong laptop. Provider keys stay server-side. | Phase 2 browser-local save only | commits efcce87, 4de1cfe; `SECURITY.md` |
| A10 | Sound rebuilt: per-scene ambience and reverb, and physical-model sound effects that follow game events. Music dips while a voice speaks. | Audio cues reinforce on-screen warnings without replacing them. Every cue is also visible. | Phase 2 optional simple tones | commit 3981e36; `src/lib/audio/` |
| A11 | Briefing redesign: objective, controls, rival portrait and quote, "In Homer / in this game", "How does the AI rival work?", difficulty with a plain-language note, the rival panel and a short practice that saves nothing. | Keeps Daniel's need for a clear objective and practice, and states plainly what the AI decides and what the game decides. | W04-W09 briefing wireframes | `src/ArcadeApp.tsx` |
| A12 | The voyage map gained a trial gallery and an illustrated voyage chart, with a per-trial AI technique note. | Faster trial choice, and the AI technique is shown before the player commits. | S03 map | `src/components/menu/` |
| A13 | Settings now group AI rival status (models in use, check connection, how to connect), commentary and voice, sound, motion and visual effects, earlier modes, and a showcase "Unlock all islands". | One place to understand and control the AI and accessibility options. | S12 settings overlay | `src/ArcadeApp.tsx` settings dialog |
| A14 | A mock AI service sits behind the single module that makes every model call. It can return success, a slow response, a low-confidence result, an error or "offline", switched from a second browser tab during a session. | The brief asks for every AI state to be shown and tested on demand. Task 4 uses it to lead participants into an AI error without warning. | Phase 2 could show failure states only by stopping the model | `server/lib/mock-ai.ts`, `app/api/ai-mock/route.ts`, `tests/mock-ai.test.mjs` |
| A15 | A separate low-confidence state, "The rival's plan was set aside". It is amber, not red, and its main action is to ask for another strategy. | Before this, a strategy that failed the game's checks looked the same as a connection error. Rosa needs to see that the game refused to use a doubtful result. | Phase 2 A1 error state only | `src/ArcadeApp.tsx` rival panel, frame D22 |
| A16 | Undo for the AI strategy. After "Ask for another strategy", the player can go back to the previous one. While a new one loads, fails or is set aside, "Keep the previous strategy" restores it. | The brief asks users to be able to edit, reject, regenerate or undo AI results. Regenerating used to discard a strategy the player might have preferred. | Phase 2 "ask again" only | `src/ArcadeApp.tsx`, frames D24, D25 |
| A17 | Design tokens: one file defines the palette (background, surface, text, muted text, one accent, an AI colour, error, success), four font families, five text sizes and one spacing scale. The older stylesheets now read them. | Style basics written down once and reused on every screen and in code, as the brief requires. | Colours spread across several stylesheets | `src/styles/tokens.css`, `docs/UI_DESIGN.md` |
